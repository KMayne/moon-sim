# Texture provenance

Both maps are equirectangular (simple cylindrical), 2:1, centred on 0° longitude, with north up.

## earth_bluemarble_4k.jpg

- **Source:** NASA Earth Observatory, Blue Marble: Next Generation (December, with topography and
  bathymetry). `world.topo.bathy.200412.3x5400x2700.jpg` from
  https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/73000/73909/world.topo.bathy.200412.3x5400x2700.jpg
  (record: https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/images/73909)
- **Original:** 5400×2700 JPEG, sRGB.
- **Licence:** NASA imagery, public domain in the US. Credit: NASA Earth Observatory / Reto Stöckli.
- **Processing:** resized to 4096×2048 (Lanczos, via sharp), re-encoded as JPEG quality 85
  (mozjpeg).

## moon_svs_4k.jpg

- **Source:** NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, CGI Moon Kit (https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720),
  colour map `lroc_color_poles_4k.tif` from
  https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a004700/a004720/lroc_color_poles_4k.tif — derived from
  LRO Wide Angle Camera mosaics.
- **Original:** 4096×2048 TIFF, 8-bit sRGB. "The map is centered on 0° longitude." (SVS page)
- **Licence:** NASA, public domain in the US. Credit: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio.
- **Processing:** re-encoded as JPEG quality 85 (mozjpeg, via sharp); no resampling.
